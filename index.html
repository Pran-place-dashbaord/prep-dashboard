The 10-Minute Daily Reading Habit

Enough to stay current. Not so much that it becomes a job in itself.

Mon & Wed

ET Brand Equity + Afaqs. Scan headlines, read 1-2 stories about brands on your target list.

Tue & Thu

Business Standard + Mint via Google News. One story on FMCG earnings or market share. Look for numbers.

Friday

Inc42 for Consumer Tech updates + Exchange4Media for FMCG trade news.

Weekend (15 min)

Campaign India or Business Today for a longer brand strategy read.

Before any interview

Search the specific brand on Campaign India. See what campaign they ran in the last 3 months. Reference it in your closing question.

💡 How to Form a Quick Opinion on Any News Item — For Interview Use

1
Name what move it is. Connect it to one of the 10 Market Actions. "Britannia launching a new premium biscuit range is a Premiumisation play." That one label shows you think in frameworks, not just facts.
2
Say whether it makes sense. One sentence. Pick a side — don't hedge with "it depends."
3
Name the risk. One sentence. "The risk is that a Rs 50 biscuit is a hard sell in a category where the reference price is Rs 10." Shows commercial maturity.
4
Connect it to something you know. Link the news item to a case study you already know. That's a very good interview answer.
ET Brand Equity
Brand & Marketing
The most directly relevant source for a marketing grad. Brand campaigns, FMCG product launches, marketer interviews, category strategy stories.
What to look for: New campaign launches by brands on your target list, CMO interviews, premiumisation and rural push stories.
Afaqs! — Marketing
Brand & Marketing
India's advertising and marketing trade publication. Campaigns, creative work, media spends, agency news. Free to read.
What to look for: Which brands are spending heavily (signals growth ambition), campaign strategy breakdowns, digital vs. traditional media trends.
Business Standard
FMCG & Business
Strategy-level brand and business stories. Strong on quarterly results, market share data, and M&A. Opens via Google News.
What to look for: FMCG earnings commentary (volume growth vs. price growth), distribution strategy shifts, companies on your target list.
Mint
FMCG & Business
Stronger on numbers. FMCG earnings, Consumer Tech fundraising, Reliance/Tata moves, quick commerce economics. Opens via Google News.
What to look for: FMCG volume vs. value growth commentary, rural demand signals, quick commerce market share updates.
Campaign India
Brand & Marketing
Advertising campaigns and brand case studies from an agency and creative perspective.
What to look for: Brand repositioning campaigns, new product launch campaigns, how major brands are changing creative strategy.
Inc42
Consumer Tech
Consumer Tech, startups, quick commerce, and growth platforms. Best source for Swiggy, Blinkit, Zepto, Reliance, PhonePe news.
What to look for: Quick commerce market share updates, funding rounds, profitability milestones — especially companies in your BDD list.

2nd Year MBA (Core) · TAPMI Manipal · Marketing & Analytics

Who I Am
I like talking to people, understanding why they buy what they buy, and finding ways to help a business grow because of that understanding. I keep coming back to four things — customers, markets, growth, and getting things done on the ground.
What I'm Curious About
1. How markets and companies work — I like figuring out why some companies do well and others don't, why customers stick to a brand or switch, and why some sectors grow faster than others. This is also why I follow the stock market and read up on business strategy and competition — not as a hobby, but because it helps me see the bigger picture.
2. Understanding the customer — Both my internships taught me the same thing in different ways — a product doesn't sell itself. What works is understanding what the customer actually needs, how they decide, what makes them trust a brand, and what they think they're getting for their money.
3. Growing things, not just running things — Given a choice, I'd rather work on getting new customers, growing into a new market, or finding a new opportunity — than just keep an existing process running smoothly. I enjoy the build/grow side more than the maintain side.
